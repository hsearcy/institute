# Wraith coherent motion v1

Status: not approved. Provisional estimates: idle 6, hit 6.5, death 6.5. These are limited evidence estimates, not final rubric scores.

Evidence copies are in `wraith-coherent-v1/`, including the actual dissolve frames in its `dissolve/` folder. The reviewer inspected idle at 0, 1.1, and 2.2 seconds; hit at 0.1, 0.3, and 0.65 seconds; solid death at 0.3, 0.65, and 1.1 seconds; dissolve death at 1.1, 1.6, and 2.2 seconds. Other supplied frames are preserved but were not inspected individually.

Staff and neck breaks are no longer visible in these frames. This is a clear integrity improvement. Idle motion is barely visible even at close scale. Hit has readable whole-form recoil but little visible cloth delay in recovery.

Death rises at 0.65 seconds, returns close to its alert standing pose at 1.1 seconds, then disappears from the top down. It reads as a hover followed by removal. A spectral death does not need a physical corpse, but it needs a visible loss of held form before disappearance.

Increase the continuous chest and cloth arc and delayed hem movement enough to read at game scale. Keep staff and head surfaces coherent. Let the silhouette soften or curl during release.
