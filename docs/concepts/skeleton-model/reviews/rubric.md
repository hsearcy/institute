# Independent skeleton model review

The requested pass mark is 8 out of 10. The review applies to the new skeleton model, not the full game. Each row has a maximum of one point. Half and quarter points are permitted when the evidence is mixed.

| Item | Full-point evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Concept and silhouette | Thin undead soldier, human proportions, readable gaps, and clear match to the reference from all four sides. |
| Skull | Shaped cranium, eye and nose cavities, cheek bones, jaw, and individual teeth. No ball head or mask face. |
| Torso | Curved open ribs, sternum, spine, clavicles, and shaped pelvis. No ladder chest. |
| Limbs | Shaped bones, joint ends, separate forearm and shin bones, distinct fingers and toes. |
| Armor | One main shoulder plate, split chest armor, helmet, forearm plates, and shin plates. Edges, straps, and damage have clear form. |
| Cloth | Teal hanging panels with folds, thickness, torn edges, and layered construction. No flat triangle skirt. |
| Materials | Bone, worn metal, leather, and cloth remain distinct in the exported file. Surface detail has more than one scale. |
| Detail and equipment | Sword, buckles, rivets, and straps fit the same visual design. No unexplained gaps or large intersections. |
| Export | The actual GLB has sound surfaces, packaged textures, useful scale, and measured size and mesh counts. |
| Runtime use | The model reads at game size. A movement test shows usable joints and no major detached parts. |

Pass requires at least 8 points and no major failure: a body made mainly from visible basic shapes; missing skull or torso anatomy; absent export evidence; missing textures; or broken movement.

Required evidence: exported GLB front, side, back, three-quarter, skull close-up, game-size view, and movement view. Source-file renders alone cannot pass. The reviewer must see all captured views. Failed views remain in the record.
