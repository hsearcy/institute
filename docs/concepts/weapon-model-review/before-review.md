# Current weapon model review

All 23 current models score below 6/10 against their concept art. Upgrade all 23. The best scores are 5.5/10 for Apprentice and Thunderbell. These scores do not preserve an earlier acceptance against a different target.

## Method

I inspected all 92 browser images: front, side, back, and three-quarter for each current live GLB. The comparison sheets below preserve those four views beside the concept. The source screenshots are in `/tmp/weapon-review-before/`. The view selector text stays at Front in those screenshots, but the model camera changes; the sheet labels identify the actual view.

The score has five parts: silhouette (3), proportions (2), material (2), concept detail (2), and visible construction (1). A score below 6 requires an upgrade. Recognizable item type alone is not sufficient. Small missing ornaments are less important than a wrong head or blade shape.

This is an isolated export review. It does not approve native grip, scale, attachment, animation, collision, or runtime cost. Those checks are still required before replacement installation under `scripts/blender/items/README.md`. Inferno, Wardenheart, Thunderbell, and Stormorbit remain worn objects. Their replacement must not change the equipment rules. File hashes in `scores.json` identify the reviewed live assets.

## Required corrections

### apprentice — 5.5/10

The guard bends down instead of sweeping up. The grip is too long relative to the blade. The plain round pommel lacks the ring detail. Bright steel lacks the dark forged surface and brass collar detail.

[Four-view comparison](before-sheets/apprentice.png)

### arcfang — 3.75/10

The blade is a zigzag strip, not a curved fang with deep edge cuts. The plain guard and hooked block pommel omit the pointed metal fittings. Purple stone and worn metal are not clear.

[Four-view comparison](before-sheets/arcfang.png)

### cinderwand — 4.75/10

The broad three-prong fork replaces the close curved ember cradle. No clear ember is visible. The lower leather grip and diagonal metal straps are missing. Bark detail is present but has little charred contrast.

[Four-view comparison](before-sheets/cinderwand.png)

### cleaver — 4/10

The blade is narrow and rounded instead of broad with a slanted squared end. The long riveted spine plate is missing. The small guard and plain pommel lack the heavy forged construction. Steel is too clean and bright.

[Four-view comparison](before-sheets/cleaver.png)

### glacier — 4/10

The smooth convex blade loses the fang cuts and inner curve. The plain short guard replaces the large swept socket. The dark flat surface lacks fractured ice. The diamond pommel and layered metal fittings are missing.

[Four-view comparison](before-sheets/glacier.png)

### hailcaller — 4.5/10

The crystal cluster is too simple and uneven. Thin prongs and a smooth cup replace the broad riveted cage. The narrow handle lacks the pointed pommel. Flat blue facets lack ice fractures and frost.

[Four-view comparison](before-sheets/hailcaller.png)

### icespear — 4/10

The broad central point and upward prongs replace the long thin point and downward ice blades. The head lacks its open metal frame. The shaft, grip bands, and butt spike are too simple. Ice is a flat blue solid.

[Four-view comparison](before-sheets/icespear.png)

### inferno — 5/10

Separate flame blocks sit on a straight band instead of forming a continuous curved crown. Dark inset metal and round ember settings are absent. Two plain rear bars replace the cloth tie and buckles. Bright gold lacks wear.

[Four-view comparison](before-sheets/inferno.png)

### moonstaff — 5/10

The crescent and pale stone identify the concept. The head is too small and its socket is too short. The back is a plain metal cap. Engraved circles, hanging charm, shaft wraps, and pointed end are missing.

[Four-view comparison](before-sheets/moonstaff.png)

### phoenix — 4.25/10

A few thin feathers replace the broad asymmetric wings and curled frame. The head and gem are too small relative to the shaft. The back has a plain gem cap. The pointed pommel, spiral grip binding, and layered collar are missing.

[Four-view comparison](before-sheets/phoenix.png)

### phoenix_talon — 4.5/10

The blade curves mainly at its tip instead of forming a long talon arc. The feathered bird guard and claw pommel are missing. The straight grip loses the curved handle. Gold covers the blade face instead of framing dark steel.

[Four-view comparison](before-sheets/phoenix_talon.png)

### skybreaker — 4.25/10

A small fork replaces the deep split and stepped blade edges. The broad wing guard is reduced to a thin bar. The purple crack and inset gems are absent. Blade faces and pommel are plain.

[Four-view comparison](before-sheets/skybreaker.png)

### starfall — 4.5/10

The blade swells into a leaf shape instead of keeping the narrow straight profile. Two smooth slots replace the rough meteor core. The hooked guard, bronze fittings, and pointed pommel are absent.

[Four-view comparison](before-sheets/starfall.png)

### stormorbit — 4.25/10

Thin orbit rings replace the heavy fitted cage. The orb is smooth purple with a sharp blue lower color break, not a fractured storm crystal. The front crest, riveted back strap, and lower point are missing.

[Four-view comparison](before-sheets/stormorbit.png)

### stormsilver — 4.25/10

[Four-view comparison](before-sheets/stormsilver.png)

## Replacement gate

Use the concept shape as the target. Retain blade curves, head size, guard form, main metal fittings, and material contrast before adding small detail. Review each exact replacement GLB from four views. Then inspect it on the player in its native attachment and action. Do not use a render-only improvement as installation approval.
