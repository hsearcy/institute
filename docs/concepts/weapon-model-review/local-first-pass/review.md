# First local candidate review

No candidate in this set passes. I inspected all four views of each of these nine exported candidates. These are new candidate scores; the browser headers still show baseline scores.

The main front shapes and relative sizes are much closer to the concepts. Complete side surfaces are visible. Thin sword blades are acceptable; they do not need the depth of a staff head. The shared failure is the surface along the side join. Pale stripes cut through leather, wood, and metal. They are visible at three-quarter angles and cannot be accepted as small highlights.
