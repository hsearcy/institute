# Boss treasure

Each completed boss encounter makes two independent server rolls, one for a Crazy Coin and one for a dime. Each chance uses the completed expedition tier: 1% at tier 1, +1 percentage point per tier through tier 10 (10%), then +0.3 percentage points per tier. The tier-based chance caps at 25% from tier 60 through tier 100. Replaying a lower tier uses that lower chance, regardless of the highest unlocked tier. Both may win. The rolls are saved in `rpg_cast_claims`; their balances and the daily boss quest are committed in the same transaction. Old receipts replay unchanged. The dime balance is an additive `profile_state.dimes` column, measured in whole dimes.

Parent journal buttons redeem one reward at a time. Dimes represent $0.10 paid by the parent; no payment service is called. Both endpoints accept a request ID and save a redemption receipt. The RPG client retains that ID after a lost response, updates only the balance and status, and preserves the open journal and scroll position. The original mine's coin endpoint remains compatible without a request ID.

New item power is rolled uniformly across a range for the played tier. With `d = tier - 1`, ordinary copies roll from `1 + floor(1.5d)` to `4 + 3d`; boss copies roll from `4 + 2d` to `12 + 4d`. Boss ranges include +4–12 at tier 1, +22–48 at tier 10, and +202–408 at tier 100. A high roll is not collapsed into a shared maximum by clamping. Existing copies are not rerolled. Every boss copy rolls one of two extra traits: opening strike (+25% damage on the first cast per room) or boss slayer (+30% damage to `enemy-0` in the boss room). Actual hit damage includes these conditions; ordinary item previews show base damage with the conditions listed separately.

Boss copies also have a 40% chance of a school-matched spell shape, with five choices per school. Neutral items can draw any of the 15 shapes. The [spell effect list](../plans/spell-effect-bonuses.md) gives each visual and combat bonus: either +30% damage or +2 targets, within the ten-target limit. Each shape lends its named spell while equipped. The server records the used shape in the cast receipt so the renderer uses the same trait. Extra visual contacts do not apply damage twice.

The original `twin-orb`, `meteor-shower`, and `split-lance` IDs are retained. Their visuals are now Twin Orbit, Molten Core, and Diamond Drill. No saved item copies are rerolled.

Temporary skill rolls are now boss-only (20% at tier 1 through 30% at tier 10), drawn from visual modifier skills. Ordinary drops keep power, school power, and extra targets. Existing skill and named-spell damage rolls remain valid and are never rerolled or removed.

Validation: 437 shared, server, and web tests; production build; `web/scripts/check-boss-rewards.mjs` uses a disposable database and Windows Brave CDP on 9222 to check desktop/phone redemptions, lost-response retry, journal retention, all three base/bonus spell effects, granted-spell UI, and victory layout. No player reward balance is changed by that browser check.


After a clear, the completion screen selects a random unlocked area other than the area just cleared, when another area is available. It keeps the played tier by default. The child can choose another area or any unlocked tier and start directly. The map and pause menu allow leaving even in room one; earned rewards remain, and leaving grants no dungeon clear. The map then offers lower tiers. Tiers unlock one at a time through 100. New encounters scale base enemy health by `1 + 0.25 × (tier - 1)`. This works with the wider item ranges: ordinary upgrades leave a gradual rise in required damage, while a strong roll can make the next tier easier. Saved active encounter health is preserved.

Checks for this flow: `web/scripts/check-next-adventure.mjs` uses a disposable save and tests desktop and phone layouts, default selection, direct starts, first-room exit, reward retention, and lower-tier starts. Shared and server tests cover exact probability boundaries, saved receipt replay, played-tier rewards, and post-tier-10 progression.


Balance verification: `bun --no-env-file scripts/check-rpg-tier-balance.ts` compares actual boss-encounter cast counts with fixed spells/skills, median carried gear, and median or top-end new power rolls. All 1,568 ordinary comparisons stayed at the same or higher cast count; top-end rolls reduced casts in 818 comparisons. These are deterministic power-only cases, not an estimate of the chance of finding an upgrade. The helper writes `artifacts/tier-balance.json`. New spell traits, forge levels, answer power, and future skill upgrades can also change the result.
