# Cinderbranch unresolved coal surface

Source wood/fork and coal remain 7.5; no export is approved. V16 introduced
three interrupted lamination bands and quieter fine grain, but the nominated
right face still looks like a nearly smooth charcoal slab in the beauty view.

The false-color diagnostic reconstructs the lip-amplitude field from the final
mesh's pre-skew radial direction. Blue means approximately zero; green 0.15
and red 0.65+. It shows that the right face is reached only by narrow, nearly
parallel bands, with most of the broad face untouched. This is a predicted
field, not proof that Boolean-created faces retain that displacement.

The subsequent control uses the actual final mesh with a neutral map-free
material under the same close camera and raking studio light. It confirms
actual relief, but only as shallow
wrinkles. Critic kept 7.5 and requested one broad broken shelf on that right
face. V17 replaces only that face's thin bands with an oblique recessed level,
a broad transition plane and an unequally chipped lip. Current mass, seated
bottom, other faces and ember slit are retained. Front and close views
completed in 33.94s; independent review remains 7.5 for coal and wood. The
right-face step is insufficiently conspicuous. Visible ray hits confirm the
correct seed region, but near-parallel normals explain the shallow crease.
V18 intersects that region with a tilted fracture plane, preserving the
remaining mass, seat and heat. Its front/close renders completed in 30.10s;
the new verdict is pending, and no export or installation is approved.
