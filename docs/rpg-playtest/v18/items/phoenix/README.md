# Phoenix Scepter source study

The first source has an unequal bronze wing cradle around a fitted garnet cup,
retaining claws, enamel root panels and a wrapped leather grip. It has no
export or public installation. Independent v1 construction/material scores
are 7: separate secondary feathers leave blunt inner paddle ends and small
attachment lumps at the upper tips. The source material is also too uniformly
satin, with a plastic-looking focal stone; construction is the first correction.

V2 unified each wing into one continuous mesh but regressed
construction/silhouette to 6.5:
the feathers became broad serrated bars. Material stayed 7. V3 restores the
primary sweep and uses two shorter secondary feathers whose small roots are
sampled from, and buried inside, that primary wing. Their separate shorter
tips sit outside the main sweep. Its head and rear views completed in 29.14s;
independent review passes shown head/rear construction at 8. Material remains
7 because bronze is uniformly satin and the focal stone resembles glazed
orange candy. V4 preserves geometry and adds actual root/edge material masks
plus a deeper garnet body. Head/front renders completed in 24.83s on the
replacement Blender process. Independent construction remains 8 and material
improves to 7.5: the garnet is deeper, but broad bronze and the socket retain
uniform satin response. A visible-surface material-field audit is next. Nothing
from this item has been exported or installed.
The transformed-vertex Tip check records
0.83607m, correcting future source metadata for parts with local transforms.
V1 and v2 images, hashes and the v2 recipe preserve both failed studies.
Approval of other installed items does not apply to this source.

V5's firmer reflection and extended gradient remained material 7.5. V6 replaces
that weak gradient with a bounded sheltered patch reaching the visible inner
wing and secondary-feather exits, plus local socket-lip treatment. Geometry
and the deep garnet are unchanged. V6 front/head now score construction 8 and
material 8. Final rear/head-back and grip leather/seating also pass 8. That
new close coverage exposed polygonal axial transitions on the pommel (7.5).
V7 interpolates its original radius/height stations continuously without
enlarging it or changing the ferrule seat. Its corrected grip view passes 8,
closing complete shown source construction/material at 8 with the unchanged
v6 head/rear. Low derivation, exact export, thumbnail and native gates remain
open. First preparation measured 56,984 source triangles and 17,610 low
triangles; no maps were baked because that exceeded the 12,000 item budget.
A candidate-only small-part reduction keeps the regular grip and reduces
the many feathers/claws that previously bypassed the size threshold. Its
defaults preserve existing items. Revised preparation measured 11,212 triangles
in six projection groups, within the item budget. Its 1K color channel completed
in 1.57 seconds of Blender execution; the immutable PNG contains 477,328
nonzero pixels and its hash is preserved in `low-v2/albedo-receipt.json`. Normal and ORM channels completed in 2.75 and 9.22 seconds of Blender
execution, with immutable PNG receipts alongside the color receipt. Exact
exported texture transfer, visual fidelity, thumbnail and native checks remain
open. No Phoenix asset is installed.

The revised raw GLB embeds all three immutable completed PNGs byte for byte.
The first export attempt encountered lazy image loading after changing the
filepath; explicitly loading and packing the verified PNGs before finish
resolved that failure without rebaking. Failure, control and successful retry
are preserved. The packed candidate is 994,884 bytes, 11,212 triangles and one
material, with structural checks passing. Exact-model visual review is pending;
this is not an art or native acceptance.

Independent exact v2 review passes construction and grip/pommel transfer at 8,
but complete material fidelity is 6.5: all six feather tips, enamel inserts
and retaining claws acquire black patches. The no-normal control removes
all those patches. The original Phoenix feather builder's closed ring winding
is inward (negative signed volume for every feather-derived part); sampled
baked tip normals point behind the tangent plane and occlusion values approach
zero. Source base colors and immutable image transfer are intact. The next
candidate reverses only those face indices, preserving every vertex and
material, then repeats source and normal-enabled export checks. No-normal
rendering is retained solely as a diagnostic, not the proposed asset.

The winding-corrected v8 source head independently preserves construction and
material at 8. Its position audit matches the previous source at one micrometer.
Corrected normal-enabled export review remains pending.
