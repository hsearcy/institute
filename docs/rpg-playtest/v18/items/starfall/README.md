interrupted shallow fuller and asymmetric swept guard. Its front and depth
views completed in 21.38 seconds of Blender execution, restoring the caller
scene. Independent scores: silhouette 7.5, construction 7, material 6.5.

forged guard root, and gradual fuller terminations into intact central steel.
