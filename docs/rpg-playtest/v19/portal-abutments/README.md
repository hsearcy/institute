# Bonded portal abutments — Blender source study

This replaces the browser study's extruded wall shape with authored stone solids adjoining the reviewed portal. It remains an uninstalled candidate: there is no optimized export or native gameplay acceptance yet.

The first Blender source scored construction 7/materials 7.5. Its continuous diagonal fracture across many courses looked like a roof. V2 confines the loss to the outer corners, retains horizontal coping, adds a distinct exposed fracture material and tightens the outer arch joint. Independent V2 review: shown materials 8, construction 7.5. The remaining visible blockers are a clean chamfer at the outer corner and a thin projecting tip near the upper arch junction; rear/hidden-side and close contact coverage also remain required.

`source-v1.png` and `source-v2.png` are actual Blender renders, not game screenshots. V2 widens framing to include the whole asset, so these are not pixel-matched controls. The recipes and measured stage output accompany each version. V1 build/render took 6.25/62.66 seconds inside Blender; V2 took 7.75/55.29 seconds. Tool wall time can be longer. Every call restored the original caller scene, `Scene`.

V3 truncates the upper projecting tip and adds a small secondary fracture facet. Its mesh audit passes for all 174 closed solids: 196,292 triangles, no boundary/nonmanifold edges or zero-area faces, and positive signed volumes. The rear render reveals a separate fit issue: wall backs at +0.69m extend behind the original arch at +0.47m, creating a broad overhung shadow. V4 makes these faces coplanar. Its front, rear and close-contact renders are now complete; independent review assigns complete shown source construction 8/materials 8. The rear crescent is resolved and the close view confirms scribed stone ends at the arch. The V4 mesh audit passes for 174 closed solids and 195,584 triangles. Build/audit took 8.65 seconds; front, rear and joint renders took 53.11, 49.98 and 55.09 seconds respectively. The wider rear framing includes the complete silhouette. All three images are source renders, not exported or native gameplay evidence.

The active source is [portal_abutments.py](../../../../scripts/blender/experiments/portal_abutments.py). It includes a mesh audit for closed solids, zero-area faces, signed volume and fracture material coverage. Those changes must be rendered and independently reviewed before acceptance; syntax validation alone does not establish visual quality.

The study reuses the accepted portal's geometry and actual material node graphs. Ordinary wall faces keep its aged limestone treatment; exposed fracture faces use a separate rougher material with restrained value variation. The source's materials depend on the hash-verified CC0 rock-face-03 maps already documented in `scripts/blender/sources/rock-face-03`.

Do not install this asset based on these source renders. The required next gates are complete source review, reduced mesh/baked texture fidelity, native integration and actual doorway traversal, including the existing cutaway behavior and performance. Global world quality remains 6.5/10.
