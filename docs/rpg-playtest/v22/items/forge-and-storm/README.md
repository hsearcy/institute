# Fire and storm weapon sources

Six distinct first sources exist: Phoenix Talon, Sunforged Runeblade, Arc Fang,
Skybreaker, Stormsilver Runeblade and Tempest Caller. All passed closed-source
topology checks before rendering. They are source studies only, not exported
or installed assets.

Independent first-view construction/material scores are respectively 6/5.5,
6.5/6, 6/5.5, 5.5/5.5, 6.5/6 and 5/6. The next targets are a continuous talon
sweep, integrated radial sun guard, unequal forged Arc Fang bend/edge,
continuous Skybreaker point shoulders, fitted basket rails, and a supported
Tempest cup with varied vane thickness. Version 2 addresses the talon curve,
unites the split-point intersection and adds a continuous stone support; its
review is pending. Source correctness does not confer whole-item appeal.

Independent v2: Talon construction 6.5 (sweep improved; material 5.5); Skybreaker 6 (tines still begin on a blunt crossbar); Tempest 6.5 (cup connected but cradle remains a plain kinked post). Materials remain about 6; none is export ready.
