This source supplies the accepted default runtime hero. Independent review scored the imported
neutral/front/back/high-camera construction 8/10. A selective metallic roughness
and normal-strength adjustment raised material presentation from 7.5 to 8 while
preserving the matte leather/cloth. The optimized moderate-pose study retained
8 for visible construction and mesh integrity. Revised cast/strike staging and
full equipped wrist/forearm attachment now retain8. The final v13 upper-finger/handle
contact correction passed 8. Actual raw and packed GLB reimport fidelity also
passed 8 in front/back and high views. Native warm/cool readability, cast/Cleave, distance-driven level travel,
rapid-answer local staging and final offset-doorway clearance each passed 8 in
the reviewed desktop evidence. Other armor variants and unreviewed presentation
conditions are not included in those scoped passes.

The source has 9 visible parts and 144,657 evaluated triangles, including over-dense eyes.
Its equipment has authored base-color, metallic, roughness and normal textures;
it has no armature. No original scene was replaced and no embedded scripts ran.

The reviewed adaptation lives in `../../experiments/authored_hero.py`,
`rig_authored_hero.py` and `bake_authored_hero.py`. The study reduces microscopic
eye density and selectively reduces other meshes to about 62,000 triangles. Its
19-bone rig adds elbows, knees and three cape sections while retaining the game
clip contract: `idle`, `walk`, `cleave`, `cast`, without horizontal root travel.
The canonical `detailed/rebuild_authored_hero.py` composes those reviewed helpers
in the production full build, independently of the legacy armor-variant pipeline.

The tiny clear corneal shell is alpha-discarded in the runtime material so the
separate authored iris stays visible without a full-scene transmission pass.
An opaque replacement was rejected for blank eyes; the final packed-GLB portrait
preserved the gaze and scored8. Geometry and all texture payloads are unchanged.
